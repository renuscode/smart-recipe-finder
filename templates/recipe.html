SOUTH INDIAN

Dosa

A traditional South Indian dish made from fermented rice and urad dal batter.

30 Minutes
Breakfast
Vegetarian

Ingredients

What you need

  • Rice 2 cups
  • Urad Dal 1 cup
  • Water As required
  • Salt To taste
  • Cooking Oil 2 tbsp

Preparation

Step-by-step method

01

Soak rice and urad dal separately for several hours.

02

Grind the soaked ingredients into a smooth batter.

03

Allow the batter to ferment overnight.

04

Heat a pan and spread the batter into a thin circular shape.

05

Cook until golden and crispy. Serve hot.

Serving Suggestion

Dosa can be served with sambar, coconut chutney or vegetable-based side dishes.
